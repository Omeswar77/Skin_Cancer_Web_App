Clinical Benchmark: The ABCDE Rule for Melanoma Detection

Standard dermatological criteria for visual evaluation of pigmented cutaneous lesions.

A

Asymmetry

One half of the lesion does not match the other half in contour or pattern.

B

Border

Edges are irregular, ragged, scalloped, notched, or poorly defined.

C

Color

Pigment variation with shades of tan, dark brown, black, red, or blue.

D

Diameter

Typically larger than 6mm (approx. the diameter of a standard pencil eraser).

E

Evolving

Any lesion that changes in size, shape, color, elevation, or causes itching/bleeding.
